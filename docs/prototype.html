Nudge

Draft of the mobile app. It plans your day around when you actually wake, leave and get home, then makes you prove each task.

Try it

  1. Tap Leaving for office. The sky changes and morning tasks move to tonight. Then tap I'm home and Physics pings you (an if-then plan).
  2. Open ⚙ Demo, drag the clock to 17:40, tap I'm home. Tonight gains time and pulls tasks in.
  3. Open a task. Try Snooze three times, Focus lock, Stake it and Avoiding?. Open Chemistry for the avoiding prompt.
  4. Drag the grip on a task to pin it. Add a 20:00 meeting in Demo and watch tasks flow around it.
  5. Add proof (upload or sample) to unlock the next task and collect XP. Flip Show on my proof wall to post it.
  6. Explore Routines (rest mode, workouts, chores, bills), Study and You (garden, goals).

Prototype only. Task breakdown here is a canned sample; the real app calls Claude. The undismissible notification is Android-only.
